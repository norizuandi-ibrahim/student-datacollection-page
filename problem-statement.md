# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
The main stakeholders are university students, lecturers, academic advisors, and university management. Students who struggle with their studies may need additional academic support.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Lecturers may find it difficult to identify students at risk of poor academic performance when monitoring many students across multiple courses. Academic information, such as attendance, assignment marks, quiz results, and submission records, may be reviewed manually, making it difficult to detect early warning signs.

## Why does it matter?
If struggling students are identified too late, they may fail courses, delay graduation, or lose motivation. An AI system could help lecturers identify potential risks earlier and provide targeted support before the situation becomes more serious.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
University students, lecturers, and academic advisors may face difficulties identifying students who are at risk of poor academic performance at an early stage. Monitoring attendance, assignment marks, quiz results, and submission records manually can be time-consuming, particularly when managing large numbers of students. As a result, students who need academic support may not receive timely assistance, increasing their risk of failing courses or delaying graduation. Therefore, an AI-powered system is needed to analyse students' academic data, predict potential academic risks, and recommend appropriate support strategies to help lecturers and academic advisors make informed decisions.
