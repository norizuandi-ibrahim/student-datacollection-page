# Requirements

## Functional requirements (what the system must DO)
1. The system shall allow lecturers to upload student academic data, including attendance records, assignment marks, quiz results, and assignment submission records.

2. The system shall analyse student academic data using a machine learning model to predict each student's academic risk level as Low, Moderate, or High.

3. The system shall display a dashboard showing student academic performance, predicted risk levels, and students who may require additional support.

4. The system shall recommend appropriate academic support activities, such as tutoring, consultation sessions, and additional learning exercises, based on the identified risk factors.

5. The system shall generate a report summarising student performance, predicted academic risks, and recommended support activities for lecturers and academic advisors.

## Non-functional requirements (how the system must PERFORM)
1. Performance: The system shall generate academic risk predictions for a dataset of up to 1,000 students within 5 seconds under normal operating conditions.

2. Security: The system shall require authorised users to log in before accessing student academic records and shall restrict access according to user roles.

3. Usability: The system shall provide a user-friendly interface that allows lecturers to upload student data, view risk predictions, and generate reports with minimal technical training.
